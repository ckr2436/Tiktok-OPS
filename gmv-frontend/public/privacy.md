Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective date: July 11, 2026
Last updated: July 11, 2026

MYUPONA operates GMV Ops, an internal commerce, content, advertising, and analytics system used by authorized MYUPONA personnel. This Privacy Policy explains how GMV Ops collects, uses, stores, discloses, and protects information, including information obtained through TikTok Shop and TikTok Business APIs.

MYUPONA 运营 GMV Ops，这是供 MYUPONA 授权人员使用的内部电商、内容、广告和分析系统。本政策说明 GMV Ops 如何处理包括通过 TikTok Shop 与 TikTok Business API 获取的信息。

1. Scope and controller

This Policy applies to GMV Ops, its web interface, API integrations, automated jobs, reporting tools, and authorized administrative operations. MYUPONA determines the purposes and means of processing within GMV Ops and acts as the data controller or business operator to the extent required by applicable law.

Questions and verified privacy requests may be sent to support@myupona.com.

2. Information we process

Depending on the enabled features and permissions, GMV Ops may process:

- Account and access information: name, username, business email address, workspace, role, account status, login session data, and access logs.
- TikTok authorization information: app identifiers, authorization scopes, encrypted access or refresh tokens, token status, expiration time, and authorization history.
- Shop and seller information: seller, shop, business, advertiser, merchant-center, and linked-account identifiers and configuration metadata.
- Product and catalog information: product identifiers, titles, images, prices, inventory or availability metadata, and related catalog attributes.
- Order and operational information: order identifiers, timestamps, status, product, quantity, revenue, fulfillment or attribution information, and limited customer or delivery information only when an enabled feature and authorized API scope require it.
- Advertising and performance information: campaign, creative, budget, spend, impression, click, conversion, order, GMV, ROAS, and optimization-event data.
- Content information: authorized videos, images, titles, captions, publishing status, asset metadata, and content-performance information.
- Technical information: IP address, browser and device metadata, request timestamps, error records, audit events, job status, and security logs.

GMV Ops is not designed to collect government identification numbers, payment-card numbers, biometric data, or medical records. Users must not upload such information unless MYUPONA has expressly approved a lawful and necessary workflow.

3. How we use information

We process information only for legitimate, authorized business purposes, including:

- authenticating users and enforcing workspace and role-based access;
- connecting authorized TikTok Shop and TikTok Business accounts;
- synchronizing shop, product, order, content, campaign, and performance data;
- creating, scheduling, publishing, monitoring, and optimizing authorized content or advertising;
- generating operational dashboards, alerts, forecasts, and reports;
- investigating errors, preventing misuse, protecting accounts, and maintaining service reliability;
- responding to verified access, correction, deletion, revocation, or export requests; and
- complying with applicable law and platform requirements.

Automated recommendations support authorized operators. Material actions remain subject to configured policies, permissions, audit logging, and human oversight appropriate to the action.

4. Legal basis and authorization

We process information under the account holder's authorization, our contractual and platform obligations, legitimate business interests in operating and securing GMV Ops, and legal obligations where applicable. API data is accessed only through approved applications, granted scopes, and active authorizations. Authorization may be revoked at any time through the relevant platform or GMV Ops administrator controls.

5. Data minimization and purpose limitation

GMV Ops requests and retains only information reasonably necessary for enabled functions. We do not use TikTok data for unrelated purposes, sell personal information, or build profiles for third-party advertising. Direct customer identifiers are excluded from AI prompts and external analytics whenever aggregate or de-identified data is sufficient.

6. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose limited information:

- to TikTok Shop, TikTok Business, or another platform when required to perform an authorized API operation;
- to infrastructure, security, communications, or AI service providers acting under our instructions and only to the extent necessary to provide the service;
- to authorized MYUPONA personnel who require access for their role; or
- when required by law, valid legal process, or to protect users, MYUPONA, TikTok, or the public.

Where AI services support reporting or optimization, GMV Ops is designed to send aggregated business metrics and operational context rather than direct customer identifiers.

7. Data location and international access

Production data is stored and processed only in locations approved for the applicable integration and disclosed in the relevant platform security review. Any remote administrative access is restricted to authorized personnel, protected by access controls, logged where technically available, and limited to operational necessity. MYUPONA will update the applicable platform and this Policy before making a material change to the approved data location.

8. Security controls

MYUPONA maintains administrative and technical safeguards proportionate to the nature of the data, including:

- HTTPS encryption for data in transit;
- AES-256-GCM encryption for OAuth application secrets and access or refresh tokens stored by GMV Ops;
- PBKDF2-SHA256 password hashing and signed, secure, HTTP-only session cookies;
- separation of platform administration from tenant workspaces;
- role-based access for owner, administrator, and member functions;
- least-privilege database accounts and local-only access to internal application data services;
- audit logging for security-sensitive and operational actions;
- restricted environment-file and backup permissions;
- routine security updates, dependency review, log review, and credential revocation or rotation when risk is detected; and
- documented incident response, data deletion, backup, and recovery procedures.

No system is completely secure. Users must protect credentials, use strong passwords, enable multi-factor authentication where available, and promptly report suspected misuse.

9. Retention and deletion

We retain information only for operational, security, contractual, and legal needs:

- temporary OAuth authorization sessions normally expire within one hour;
- authorization tokens and linked-account records are retained until revoked, deleted, expired, or no longer required;
- high-frequency campaign metrics and snapshots are normally retained for up to 90 days, while daily performance records may be retained for up to 730 days for trend analysis and accountability;
- uploaded or generated temporary artifacts use feature-specific expiration periods;
- protected database backups are retained on a rolling 14-day schedule; and
- order, customer, content, audit, and operational records are deleted or de-identified when no longer necessary, following a verified request, or at the end of the applicable relationship unless law requires retention.

When API access is revoked or a verified deletion request is approved, MYUPONA will delete or securely remove applicable tokens and related data from active systems and allow residual backup copies to expire under the backup schedule.

10. Individual and seller rights

Subject to applicable law and identity verification, an individual, seller, or authorized platform representative may request:

- access to relevant personal information;
- correction of inaccurate information;
- deletion or restriction of information;
- a portable copy where applicable;
- revocation of an authorization; or
- information about processing and retention.

Send requests to support@myupona.com. We may request information necessary to verify identity, authority, account, and request scope. We will assist TikTok Shop and authorized sellers with valid data-subject requests and respond within the period required by applicable law or platform policy.

11. Security incidents and notification

Suspected incidents are escalated to the MYUPONA system owner for triage, containment, evidence preservation, credential rotation or revocation, recovery, and corrective action. When an incident affects platform or seller data, MYUPONA will notify TikTok, affected sellers, regulators, or individuals as required by contract, platform policy, and applicable law.

12. Children

GMV Ops is a business operations system and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the service.

13. Policy changes

We review this Policy at least annually and after material changes to processing, security controls, law, or platform requirements. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
